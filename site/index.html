Our mission

To nurture the next generation of values-oriented, culturally grounded therapists, and to strengthen resilience in the communities we serve.

Compassion, respect for nature and animals, and a commitment to community are part of everything we do. We work toward a more peaceful world across cultures.

What we offer

  • Culturally responsive psychotherapy
  • Spiritually integrated counselling that respects your values and religion
  • Public mental health workshops, community programs and community support
  • Clinical supervision and professional training for therapists from diverse backgrounds
