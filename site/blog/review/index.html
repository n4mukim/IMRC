New posts and edits to live posts, oldest first. You cannot review your own work: another reviewer or an admin does that. Check posts against IMRC’s rules: no testimonials, client stories, promised results, comparisons or superlatives, no personal health information, exact professional titles.

Trusted authors publish without review. Spot-check what went live, and unpublish anything that breaks the rules (the author is told why).
