Approaches

How practitioners work

Approaches are listed only after a practitioner attests to their training and an administrator reviews it. Training levels are shown on each profile.

AEDP

Accelerated Experiential Dynamic Psychotherapy. An experiential approach that pays close attention to emotion and body sensation, moment by moment, within the therapy relationship.

ACT

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. Builds psychological flexibility by clarifying what matters to you and practising mindful, committed action.

DBT

Dialectical Behaviour Therapy. A skills-based approach that teaches mindfulness, emotion regulation, distress tolerance and interpersonal effectiveness.

Mindfulness-based

Uses present-moment awareness practices to change how we relate to thoughts, emotions and sensations.

Sandplay

Uses a tray of sand and small figures to express inner experience, without needing words.

Spiritual Care

Spiritually integrated psychotherapy

Spiritual care is integrated within Registered Psychotherapy sessions, and only when you want it. Non-clinical spiritual care is also available as a separate session on request.

Your values, religion and spiritual life are welcome in the room. They are yours to bring, or to leave out.

Before you book

What to know before you book

A complimentary 15-minute phone or video consultation is offered as a first step.

What does a session cost?
Individual sessions are $225 CAD for 50 minutes. Couples and family sessions are $325 CAD for 75 minutes. A limited number of sliding-scale spots are available. A complimentary 15-minute phone or video consultation is offered first.
Is psychotherapy covered by OHIP or insurance?
Psychotherapy with a Registered Psychotherapist is not covered by OHIP. Many extended health benefit plans do cover Registered Psychotherapists, so check your own plan. There is no direct billing: official receipts are issued through Jane App for reimbursement. [Confirm: no direct billing]
Online or in person?
Secure video sessions are available across Ontario. In-person sessions take place at the Toronto office, by appointment on designated days. [Confirm office location: Toronto or Markham]
What if I live outside Ontario?
Clients must be physically located in Ontario during sessions, under CRPO regulations.
What is the cancellation policy?
24 hours' notice is required. Late cancellations and no-shows are billed the full session fee.
Who regulates Registered Psychotherapists?
The College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO) sets the rules for Registered Psychotherapists and handles complaints.
